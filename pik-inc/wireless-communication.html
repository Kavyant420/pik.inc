Wireless Communication

Send messages between nearby PIK devices over Bluetooth — no cellular plan, no Wi-Fi uplink, no access point that could log you. The radio does the work instead of the tower.

How it stays anonymous

Idle. No radios have been touched yet.

Mesh status: healthy

Zero data consumption

Messages travel the last few metres by Bluetooth LE instead of riding a carrier network — ideal when plans are metered, congested or monitored.

Short-range by design

A ~10–30 metre envelope is exactly what makes the channel hard to surveil: an observer needs to be standing next to both devices.

Hash-routed identity

Peers exchange rotating SHA-256 aliases rather than phone numbers, so a captured packet maps back to a relay record — never to you.
