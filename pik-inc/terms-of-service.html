Accounts & local identities

PIK accounts are device-local by design. You are responsible for safeguarding the device holding your digests; we cannot reset a password we never see, and no support channel can reconstruct what we do not store. Handles are chosen freely — see Acceptable Use for limits.

The PIK suite

  • Pikuflix streams licensed content; playback entitlements attach to your local session hash.
  • PIK AI, PIKMAPS, PIKTURE process queries locally where feasible; sync is optional and always digest-first.
  • PIK VPN routes through public relays; free service assumes shared-bandwidth fairness.
  • PIKOS & PIK IDE are developer tools shipped as-is during preview phases.
  • Wireless Communication requires compatible Bluetooth hardware; range limitations are inherent and disclosed.

Acceptable use

Do not use PIK products to violate law, infringe rights, distribute malware, or evade lawful network administration. Anonymity tooling protects private individuals — it is not a licence to harm others. We may block specific client installations that demonstrably abuse the relay fleet while preserving user privacy elsewhere.
