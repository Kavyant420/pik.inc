Privacy Policy

Effective 1 Oct 2026Version 3.2

What we collect

Very little, and never in plain text. PIK products are engineered so that a complete breach of our servers would yield no usable identity data.

  • Hashed credentials. Passwords become PBKDF2-SHA256 digests with a random per-account salt; email addresses become one-way SHA-256 digests used only to recognise your own account locally.
  • Local activity records. Interactions inside PIK apps are hashed into an activity ledger stored in your browser's local storage. The ledger attributes each record to the exit IP of a public relay chain — Freedom-1, FallExit-A, TorNode-3, Privoxy-NY, Anonize-SV, NordPass-7, Cloaknet-X, GuardUML-X or OpenHop-R — rather than to you.
  • Preferences. Theme, density, motion and audio settings persist on your device.

What we explicitly do not collect

  • No raw email addresses, phone numbers or mailing addresses.
  • No third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, session replay or fingerprinting scripts.
  • No location history: PIKMAPS routes expire from memory the moment you arrive.
  • No content metadata from Wireless Communication messages, which travel device-to-device over Bluetooth without touching carrier networks.
Because these data never exist, they cannot be subpoenaed, leaked or sold. That is the entire privacy strategy in one sentence.

Anonymity & relay routing

Every recorded action carries the address of the relay exit that served it at the time. Chains rotate through three hops drawn from well-known public relay networks, refreshed on demand from Settings → Privacy. Anyone examining a record sees a shared relay endpoint used by many unrelated users, not a personal identifier.

Relay attribution is a transparency mechanism, not a guarantee of untraceability against a determined local adversary; pair it with PIK VPN for hostile networks.

Cookies & local storage

PIK sets no cookies. State lives in localStorage scoped to this browser: identity hashes, route chains, preference blobs and waitlist digests. Blocking storage degrades convenience features but never breaks core functionality — every product must ship able to run with storage disabled.

Data deletion

You control erasure completely:

  • Settings → Danger zone → Purge deletes alias hashes, the activity ledger, route chains, accounts and sessions instantly.
  • Clearing browser storage achieves the same result; there is no server-side copy to request removal of.
  • Pikuflix watchlists and PIK IDE workspaces live only on the device that created them.

Deletion requests sent to our mailbox can only be acknowledged, because there is nothing on our side to delete — a design choice we consider the more honest answer.

Security measures

  • Content-Security-Policy headers forbid inline remote scripts; referrer is disabled site-wide.
  • All cryptographic operations run client-side via WebCrypto with a pure-JS fallback for older engines.
  • Wireless Communication handshakes exchange rotating aliases instead of persistent identifiers.
  • PIKOS enforces hashing at the syscall layer for products shipped on it.

Changes to this policy

Material changes are announced in-app at least 30 days before taking effect, with a diff view in Settings. Continued use after the notice period constitutes acceptance.
