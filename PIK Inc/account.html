Sign in or sign up — both run entirely in your browser. Passwords are hashed with PBKDF2-SHA256, emails become opaque digests, and nothing is ever transmitted to PIK.

Your password is stretched with PBKDF2-SHA256 (120,000 iterations) in this browser. Only the salt and digest touch storage — and both stay on your device.

Empty

No email verification, no server round-trip: PIK cannot mail you because PIK never sees your address — only its one-way hash.

Why no real login?

A centralised password database is a breach waiting to happen. By keeping accounts device-local, the worst-case leak is one machine — and even then only digests exist.

What if I lose this device?

Local-only means there is no recovery service we could safely offer; that trade-off is deliberate. Settings → Privacy lets you purge everything before parting with hardware.

Is any telemetry collected?

None leaves your browser. The activity log on the Security page is assembled from hashes of your own clicks, traced to rotating public relay IPs.
