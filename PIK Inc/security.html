What we store about you

  • Passwords: random 16-byte salt + PBKDF2-SHA256 digest, kept in this browser's localStorage only.
  • Emails: converted to a SHA-256 digest with domain masking; the raw address never leaves your keyboard.
  • Activity: each interaction becomes a hash record attributed to the current relay exit IP.
  • Preferences: theme, audio and density settings live beside the hashes — equally local, equally purgeable.

ANONYMITY LAYER

Traced to relays, not to you

Every PIK action is stamped with the exit point of a three-hop chain through well-known public relays — Freedom-1, FallExit-A, TorNode-3 and friends that already serve millions of users around the clock. Anyone inspecting the ledger sees relay addresses, never yours.

Threat model, honestly

  • Who we defend against: network observers, breached databases, subpoena-style requests to PIK.
  • What we cannot promise: protection from a compromised device or a malicious browser extension.
  • Mitigation: strict CSP, no third-party scripts, referrer disabled, all crypto runs client-side.

Engineering commitments

  • No analytics SDK may ever ship in a PIK product.
  • Any new feature must function with cookies and storage blocked.
  • Public relays are named openly so audits can reproduce routing claims.
